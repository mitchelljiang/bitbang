# About me

Hi, my name is Mitchell, and I am a 4th year Computer Engineering student at the Georgia Institute of Technology. I love designing embedded software and hardware, specifically consumer electronics. I have previous work experience interning at Apple and the Naval Postgraduate School, and I am currently a teaching assistant for ECE 4180: Embedded System Design.

## Why bitbang exists

With AI becoming more widespread, it can be easy to gloss over the low-level fundamentals. This is meant to be a tool to help students learn how things work, not just what they are. I plan to add visualizations for bit manipulation and computer architecture, but communication protocols are something I am especially interested in, so I am focusing on those first ;)
